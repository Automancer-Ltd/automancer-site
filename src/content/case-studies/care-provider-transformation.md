## A care business held together by spreadsheets and goodwill

Supported-living care runs on paperwork: rosters, supervisions, medication records, training compliance, incident logs, mileage claims, and timesheets. This provider ran most of it by hand across three branches, using separate systems and spreadsheets that could not share data.

That creates gaps wherever a regulator looks. Inspection evidence sat in different tools. Refresher training was tracked in spreadsheets. Managers could only spot care-quality patterns when someone had a spare afternoon to compare exports. Around 500 support workers did not even have a company email address to sign in with.

They needed their daily systems connected in a sensible order, without disrupting care along the way.

## Delivered in ~18 staged projects

Rather than risk one giant launch, we split the work into separate projects. Each one is scoped, tested against real exports, and signed off before it touches live client records.

### The operations platform (ERP / CRM)

We built one system to replace a recruitment-only tool and dozens of spreadsheets. It holds one clean record for every carer and resident, tracks referrals from first call to onboarding, and manages incidents, purchase requests, and daily tasks. Every change is logged so managers can show an inspector who did what and when.

Results from the live pilot:

- One roster import created **294 carers and 293 service users** as clean records straight from the client's CSV files.
- Identity matching **resolved 274 of 418** possible duplicates automatically, leaving only the genuinely unclear ones for staff to check.
- **588 supervision and spot-check schedules** were created automatically.
- Every report was checked against the client's existing exports — 500 visits, 175 medication alerts, and 416 names flagged for review — so managers could trust the figures from day one.

The platform is live in a production pilot that the client uses and reviews daily.

### Self-hosted training and compliance system

We replaced outsourced training and spreadsheet tracking with a training system the provider runs itself. A custom compliance view tracks mandatory, refresher, and overdue training across all three branches. Instead of gathering CQC inspection evidence from several tools, managers can show an inspector one screen.

### Management oversight dashboard

This dashboard is live. It brings care-quality alerts from separate systems into one view: branch comparisons, priority watchlists for support workers and residents, medication reviews, and an action queue. Managers see what needs attention in one place instead of checking three systems.

### Monitored, EU-hosted infrastructure

Everything runs on private EU-hosted servers with automated monitoring, encrypted backups, and a tested restore drill. The production audit passed with **zero failures and zero warnings across every layer.**

### Support-worker login accounts

Roughly 500 support workers had no consistent login, which blocked the training and staff portal rollout. We gave every worker a managed, EEA-hosted mailbox with automated setup tools so staff can sign in safely.

**Also in build:** a staff portal (announcements, policies, HR resources, clock-in/out), a mobile mileage-claims app that checks routes and rates automatically, and digital leave requests. Alongside that, we delivered reviews of Microsoft 365 access, AI rota scheduling, phone call transcription, and staff WhatsApp announcements.

## Checked against real data before go-live

Every project is built on a test system first, checked against the client's real exports, and signed off before it touches live resident data. The client tests each part and gives feedback before launch.
